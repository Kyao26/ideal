 BLOK 1 - KARAKTER GOSTERGESI (teshis, ayri panel)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Cizgiler: Hrs | Sfr(0.5) | optA | optH | pB

 optH = optA * kok(pB) -> ufuk duzeltmeli optA. Eksik olan carpan buydu:
 SgR, 2 periyotluk VIDYA'ya gore 1 BARLIK takip hatasini olcuyor; oysa
 bandin korumasi gereken sey elde tutma ufku boyunca birikmis geri
 cekilme. R/S yasasi: N barlik sapma = 1 barlik sapma * N^H. N=pB ve
 H=0.5 alindiginda carpan kok(pB) olur.
 BEKLENEN: optH medyani ~1.1-1.5, yani sizin klasik 1.4'unuze yakin.
 BU BIR HIPOTEZ - once bunu olcun, dogrulanmadan uretime almayin.
------------------------------- KOPYALA ---------------------------------------

N1:=(HHV(H,10)-LLV(L,10))/10;
N2:=(Ref(HHV(H,10),-10)-Ref(LLV(L,10),-10))/10;
N3:=(HHV(H,20)-LLV(L,20))/20;
Rr0:=If(N3>0 AND N1+N2>0,(N1+N2)/N3,2);
Rr:=If(Rr0<1,1,If(Rr0>4,4,Rr0));
Hr0:=2-Log(Rr)/Log(2);
Hrs:=If(Hr0<0.2,0.2,If(Hr0>0.9,0.9,Hr0));
pB:=If(Rr<2.171,2,If(Rr<2.287,3,If(Rr<2.432,5,If(Rr<2.593,8,If(Rr<2.774,13,21)))));
C1:=Mov(C,2,VAR);
Rz:=(C-C1)/C;
Mn:=Sum(Rz,20)/20;
SgR:=1.2533*Sum(Abs(Rz-Mn),20)/20;
op0:=100*SgR/Hrs;
optA:=If(op0<0.05,0.05,If(op0>10,10,op0));
kH:=If(pB=2,1.4142,If(pB=3,1.7321,If(pB=5,2.2361,If(pB=8,2.8284,If(pB=13,3.6056,4.5826)))));
optH:=optA*kH;
Sfr:=Hrs*0+0.5;
Hrs;Sfr;optA;optH;pB;
